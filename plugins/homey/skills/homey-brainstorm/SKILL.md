---
name: homey-brainstorm
description: Run a brainstorm or discussion with the user without losing or chasing side ideas.
---

## Who runs it
Main session.

## Steps
1. Stay on the user's topic. Hold your own ideas until they ask.
2. The user raises a new idea, later topic or to-do: start `capturer` in the background with `homey-capture`. Say nothing about it beyond a short mark; do not stop the discussion or wait for it.
3. When the discussion ends, read what came back, check the notes exist, and show one table: item, where it went.
4. Log it with `homey-log`.
